# Pilot results

Generated by `python pilot/report.py` from `pilot/results/llm_bits.jsonl` and `pilot/results/baselines.json`.
See the [README](../README.md) for how the numbers were measured and for their limits.

- Unit: bits per byte of the original verse-per-line file, verse references included. Lower is better.
- Translation ids are eBible.org ids. Models are `Qwen3.5-0.8B-Base`, `Qwen3.5-2B-Base` (pre-trained only) and `Qwen3.5-0.8B` (chat-tuned).
- Model weights are not counted in any LLM figure.
- LLM figures are ideal arithmetic-coding costs on sampled chapters, not sizes of compressed files.
